## Procedure

1. **Frame** — product, segment, decision to inform (copy, SEO, CRO, lead magnet).
2. **ICP sketch** — who buys, who blocks, trigger event, budget owner.
3. **Jobs / pains** — primary job-to-be-done, current workaround, cost of status quo.
4. **Language mine** — phrases from reviews, tickets, sales calls, Reddit/community (cite sources). Prefer customer words over marketing words.
5. **Objections map** — top 5 objections + proof that kills each.
6. **Messaging brief** — one-pager: ICP, promise, proof, CTA, do-not-say list.
7. Hand off: `marketing-copywriting` / `marketing-ai-seo` / `marketing-cro` / `marketing-lead-magnets`.

## Output minimum

ICP + 5 objections + messaging brief. No fluff personas.
