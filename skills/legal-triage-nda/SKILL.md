# LEGAL — Triage NDA (NDA Triage)

**Department:** LEGAL

**NEW** procedure for fast NDA screening.

## Hard rules

1. **Not a lawyer.** Not legal advice.
2. **HIGH risk → escalate human counsel.**
3. **Never invent jurisdiction-specific law as fact.**

## Inputs

NDA text, disclosing vs receiving posture, deal context.

## Procedure

1. Classify mutual vs one-way; term; survival; definition of Confidential Info.
2. Flag red patterns: perpetual non-compete disguised as NDA, overbroad residuals, unilateral ownership of feedback, uncapped liability oddities, missing residual/standard exclusions.
3. Score go / negotiate / stop — with reasons.
4. Produce 5–10 counsel questions if negotiate/stop.

## Output

Triage card: type, term, top risks, recommendation. No "this NDA is fine" without caveats.
