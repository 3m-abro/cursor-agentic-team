# LEGAL — Risk Assessment

**Department:** LEGAL

**NEW** procedure for structured risk registers.

## Hard rules

1. **Not a lawyer.** Not legal advice.
2. **HIGH risk → escalate human counsel.**
3. **Never invent jurisdiction-specific law as fact.**

## Inputs

Decision/project description, parties, data/money/IP at stake, known constraints.

## Procedure

1. List risk scenarios (contract, regulatory, IP, employment, product liability, reputational).
2. For each: likelihood / impact (L/M/H), early warning, mitigation, owner.
3. Identify decisions that require counsel before proceed.
4. Prefer canvas risk matrix for deliverable.

## Output

Risk register + "do not proceed without counsel" list.
