# FINANCE — Close Management (The Closer)

**Department:** FINANCE

**NEW** procedure. Prefer canvas for close calendar / RACI.

> **Disclaimer:** Not legal or financial advice. Operational close planning only.

## Inputs

Close type (month/quarter/year), entity, calendar, known dependencies (payroll, AP, revenue cutoff).

## Procedure

1. Build close checklist by day (T-5 → T+5 typical): cutoffs, accruals, recs, statements, review.
2. Assign owner per task; mark critical path.
3. Soft-close vs hard-close criteria; freeze rules.
4. Track blockers daily; link to `finance-reconciliation` / `finance-journal-entry` / `finance-variance-analysis`.
5. Exit criteria: recs done, statements draft, variance narrative, management sign-off placeholder.
6. Canvas: checklist board + red/yellow/green.

## Output

Close plan + status snapshot. Do not declare books "final" without human sign-off.
