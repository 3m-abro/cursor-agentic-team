# SOCIAL — YouTube Thumbnail

**Department:** SOCIAL

Thin dual wrap: layout craft + image generation.

## Upstream

1. Visual / composition / banner craft:
   `/home/maqsood.a@scicom.msc/.claude/skills/banner-design/SKILL.md`
2. Image generation when assets missing:
   `/home/maqsood.a@scicom.msc/.claude/skills/nano-banana-pro-openrouter/SKILL.md`

## Procedure

1. **Promise** — one idea the thumbnail sells (matches video title intent).
2. **Composition** — face or product dominant; ≤6 words on-image; high contrast.
3. **Follow banner-design** for hierarchy, safe margins, export sizes.
4. **Generate / edit** via nano-banana when stock/assets insufficient.
5. **A/B** — ship 2 variants differing in emotion or text, not both.

## Specs (default)

- YouTube: 1280×720, readable at mobile size.
- Avoid clutter; no fake UI chrome unless product demo.

## Output

Thumbnail brief + asset(s) + alt text. Brand colors from `design-brand` / voice card.
