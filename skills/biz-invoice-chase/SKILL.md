# BIZ — Invoice Chase

**Department:** BIZ

**NEW** procedure for polite, escalating collections ops.

## Inputs

Invoice list (amount, due, customer, prior contact), payment terms, tone constraints.

## Procedure

1. Age AR (current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+); prioritize by $ × days.
2. Draft chase ladder: reminder → firm → final → escalate (pause work / legal handoff).
3. Personalize each note with invoice #, amount, due date, pay link/instructions.
4. Log recommended next action + owner + date.
5. Flag disputes → pause chase; gather facts; hand LEGAL if contract dispute.

## Output

Aging table + email/message templates per stage. No harassment tone. No fake late fees.
